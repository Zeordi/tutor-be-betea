# Privacy Policy – Tutor Be Betea

**Last updated:** August 2026

Tutor Be Betea ("we", "our", or "us") operates the Tutor Be Betea mobile application and website.

## 1. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information:

- **Account Information**: Full name, phone number, email address, role (Parent or Teacher)
- **Profile Information**: Bio, subjects, rates, education details, location
- **Identity Documents**: National ID, passport, degree certificates, and liveness selfies (stored in an encrypted vault)
- **Usage Data**: Session attendance, chat messages, progress reports
- **Device & Location Data**: Approximate location for matching and geofenced attendance

## 2. How We Use Your Information

- To create and manage your account
- To match parents with verified tutors
- To process payments and hold funds in escrow
- To verify tutor identity and qualifications
- To improve safety through anti-poaching protection and attendance verification
- To send important notifications

## 3. Document Vault & Sensitive Data

Identity documents and academic certificates are encrypted using AES-256 and stored in a private vault.  
These documents are **only accessible to authorized platform administrators** for verification purposes and are never shown publicly.

## 4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data.  
We may share limited data with:
- Payment providers (Telebirr, CBE Birr, etc.) to process transactions
- SMS providers to send verification codes
- Service providers who help us operate the platform (under strict confidentiality)

## 5. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active.  
You may request deletion of your account and associated data by contacting support.

## 6. Your Rights

You have the right to:
- Access your personal data
- Correct inaccurate data
- Request deletion of your account
- Withdraw consent where applicable
