# Response Targets

## SLA Table

| Priority | Channel | First Response | Resolution |
|----------|---------|---------------|------------|
| Critical (safety/dispute) | In-app + email | 1 hour | 4 hours |
| High (billing/escrow) | In-app + email | 2 hours | 24 hours |
| Medium (technical) | In-app + email | 4 hours | 48 hours |
| Low (content/feature) | Email | 24 hours | 5 days |

## Definitions

- **First Response:** Initial human acknowledgment from support.
- **Resolution:** Issue closed or actionable workaround delivered.
- **Business hours:** Monday–Friday, 08:00–18:00 EAT (UTC+3).

## Notes

- Safety-critical tickets should bypass normal queue and alert on-call staff immediately.
- Escrow and payout issues default to **High** priority.
- Email channel is used when in-app delivery fails or for low-priority items only.
