# Cookie Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-25

Tutor Be Betea ("we", "our", "us") uses minimal client-side storage to keep the Service functional and secure. This Cookie Policy explains what we store, why we store it, and how you can control it.

## 1. What We Store

| Storage Item | Purpose | Expiry |
|-------------|---------|--------|
| Auth token (`token`) | Keeps you signed in so you don't have to re-authenticate on every page | Until you log out or the session expires |
| Refresh token (`refresh_token`) | Used to obtain new access tokens without re-entering your password | Until you log out or the session expires |
| User role (`role`) | Determines which app layout and permissions to show | Until you log out or the session expires |
| Theme preference | Remembers your preferred color scheme across sessions | 365 days |

## 2. No Third-Party Tracking Cookies

We do **not** currently set advertising, marketing, or analytics cookies (e.g., Google Analytics, Facebook Pixel). Any optional error tracking (Sentry) runs via API and does not use browser cookies.

## 3. Managing Your Data

You can clear stored tokens at any time by logging out from the app. Clearing your browser's local storage or site data will also remove theme preferences and auth tokens.

## 4. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy if we introduce new storage mechanisms. The "Last updated" date reflects the most recent revision.

For questions about this Cookie Policy, contact us at legal@tutorbebetea.com.
